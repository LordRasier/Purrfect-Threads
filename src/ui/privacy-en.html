Privacy policy

Closed-test policy — October 4, 2026. Purrfect Threads by Auraliax can be played without an account. Optional Android Google accounts and purchases depend on the build and service availability. Android includes Google AdMob banners; the web version has no advertising SDK.

On-device progress

Workshop progress, companions, achievements and their read status, upgrades, language, audio and quality preferences, and timestamps stay on your device, with a local recovery copy. Auraliax does not upload or synchronize your saved game. Companion messages are prewritten, selected on your device, and are not a messaging service.

Android advertising and privacy choices

When enabled, Google's advertising services process IP addresses, approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics and identifiers to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Test ads do not mean that no network data is processed. Advertising data is separate from your saved game.

Required privacy choices are requested before ads. Where applicable, reopen them through Advertising privacy in Settings. The game remains playable without an internet connection or when an ad cannot load. Advertising is not required to unlock progress.

Google controls the retention and handling of data it receives. See Google's privacy policy. Device privacy settings provide additional advertising controls.

Optional Google account and purchases

You can play without an account. Android closed-test builds may offer optional Google sign-in and Google Play purchases; availability depends on the build, Google Play and the verification service. Connecting creates or accesses a Firebase Authentication identity in Auraliax's shared Firebase project. Firebase processes the UID, IP address, authentication/device information and Google-provided email and profile information, which may include a display name or photo. The game interface does not display that profile, but this does not mean the authentication provider receives no personal information.

Firebase App Check / Play Integrity processes app/device integrity signals and attestation tokens to protect purchase verification. Native authentication and attestation tokens travel over HTTPS to www.auraliax.com; they are not exported in saved games. The purchase ledger uses a package-specific HMAC of the Firebase UID, not your email or profile, to associate Google Play purchase history, encrypted purchase tokens, token hashes, confirmation/expiry times and consumption/revocation status. Google handles checkout; Auraliax does not receive your payment-card details. These services do not synchronize game progress.

A previously verified purchase can restore the already connected identity on this device without showing a new account picker. The native encrypted cache binds the original 12-hour grant to this device session and clock; after device restart or a clock anomaly an online verification is required. While offline, refund updates may be delayed until reconnection, at most the remaining original 12-hour grant. Native settlement prevents exported saves from replaying paid time; a crash during settlement can lose uncommitted bonus earnings.

Retention and request handling

The current service has no automatic expiry-based purge: authentication and purchase records remain indefinitely unless a verified deletion request is fulfilled. Expiry of a 12-hour boost does not delete its records. Following verification and payment settlement, the operator removes unnecessary encrypted purchase tokens and revokes Purrfect grants, including expired grants. Minimum anti-replay records remain: token hashes, the pseudonymous account HMAC and its account row, original confirmation/expiry times, and consumed/revoked status. These prevent an old paid token from granting again and preserve the ledger's integrity; the scheduling field is reset. This is pseudonymous retained data, not anonymous data.

These minimum records currently have no fixed deletion date and are kept while the purchase service can accept old tokens; retirement requires a reviewed replay-safe migration or shutdown. Pending or unresolved purchases must be settled before their tokens can be removed. Support explains any remaining restriction. Firebase identity deletion is separately checked to avoid deleting access to another Auraliax game. Server backups need operator review; no automatic backup-erasure deadline is promised. A restricted deletion record is kept while affected backups remain restorable so deletion can be reapplied before a restore serves users.

Request account deletion

Request account deletion

To request deletion of your Purrfect Threads account and associated data, email rasier_k@hotmail.com with the subject “Purrfect Threads account deletion”, preferably from your connected Google email. You do not need to reinstall or open the game. If you cannot use that address, explain this so support can arrange ownership verification. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, authentication tokens, purchase tokens or card details.

Support verifies ownership and the request's scope before acting; email alone is not automatic proof. Tell us whether you also use Jumper Jack and whether any purchase is unresolved. We explain outstanding payment steps, retained anti-replay records and the result by email. This is a manual request, not instant deletion. A Purrfect-only request does not delete your Google account or Jumper Jack progress. A Firebase identity used only for Purrfect is deleted after verification; a shared identity is retained for the other game unless a separate, explicitly authorized request covers it.

Disconnect only signs out on this device and clears its purchase cache; it does not delete Firebase or server data. Clearing app storage removes local progress and preferences, not server records; delete exported saves separately. Server revocation cannot instantly reach an offline device: a previously verified boost may last for the remainder of its original 12-hour period. Disconnect or clear storage on your devices to remove that local cache.

Backups and deletion

Export creates a progress file that you choose where to store or share. Import reads only the selected file. Clearing app storage removes local progress and preferences; delete exported copies separately. Android system backup and device transfer of app-private data are disabled. Disconnect signs out on this device and clears the local purchase cache; it does not delete the Firebase account or server purchase records.

Support and contact

Support emails and attachments will be deleted from our mailbox within 90 days after the request closes. You may request earlier deletion.
