Privacy policy

Review draft — September 25, 2026. This describes the current game. The Android application is in preparation; review this information against the final build before publication.

Purrfect Threads is a single-player game by Auraliax that does not require an account. Jumper Jack accounts, leaderboards and advertising are not part of this game.

On-device progress

The current game stores workshop progress, companions, achievements, upgrades, language, audio and quality preferences, and timestamps used to calculate progress while away. It keeps a local recovery copy. These data are not sent to an Auraliax server or synchronized with an account.

No integrated advertising or measurement

The reviewed game includes no ads, in-app purchases, analytics tools or crash-reporting services. It does not request your name, email, location, camera, microphone or contacts to play.

Google Play purchase

The planned Android app purchase will be processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. Auraliax does not request payment-card details inside the game. This page does not replace the store's information about its own data processing.

Backups and deletion

Export creates a progress file that you choose where to store or share. Import reads the selected file to restore progress. Clearing game storage removes local game data; delete exported copies separately. There is no Purrfect Threads account to close.

Support and contact

When you email rasier_k@hotmail.com, you share your email address and message so your request can be handled. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information. Use the same contact for questions or requests about data you have sent.

Support emails, including your email address, message content and attachments, will be deleted from our mailbox within 90 days after the request is closed. You may request earlier deletion using the same contact.

Updates

Any new service or feature that changes data use requires an update before release. Android backup and storage configuration must be confirmed when the installed application is finalized.
